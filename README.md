 Madhav Store – Ecommerce Sales Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard that analyses the sales performance of **Madhav Store**, an ecommerce business selling clothing, furniture and electronics across India. This repository contains the dashboard and a short presentation summarising its findings.

 
 Key Metrics

 Metric      -     Value 

 Total Amount (Sales) - ₹438K 
 Total Quantity Sold - 6K units 
 Total Profit - ₹37K 
 Profit Margin - ~8.4% 
 Sum of AOV - 121K 

 What the Dashboard Shows

- Sum of Profit by Sub-Category – which products earn the most
- Profit by Month – monthly profit trend across the year
- Sum of Quantity by Category – Clothing, Furniture and Electronics split
- Sum of Quantity by Payment Mode – COD, UPI, EMI, Debit Card and Credit Card
- Sum of Amount by Customer Name – amount share for customers Harivansh, Madhav, Madan Mohan and Shiva
- Sum of Amount by State – sales by state: Maharashtra (102K), Madhya Pradesh (87K), Uttar Pradesh (38K) and Delhi (23K)
- Filters (slicers) – Quarter (Qtr 1 to Qtr 4) and State

 Key Insights

- Top profit sub-categories: Printers (8.6K), Bookcases (6.5K), Saree (4.1K) and Accessories (3.4K).
- **Best months:** October (6.5K), January and March (6.3K each) and August (6.1K).
- **Loss-making months:** May, June, July and December. July was the weakest at -1.9K.
- **Category mix:** Clothing makes up 63% of quantity sold, with Electronics at 21% and Furniture at 17%.
- **Payment mode:** Cash on Delivery is the most common at 44%, followed by UPI (21%), Debit Card (13%), Credit Card (12%) and EMI (10%).
- **Top states:** Maharashtra (102K) and Madhya Pradesh (87K) together bring in about 43% of total sales. Uttar Pradesh (38K) and Delhi (23K) are much smaller.
- **Margin:** Sales are healthy, but only about 8% of sales turns into profit.

 Recommendations

1. Look into the loss-making months (May, June, July, December) for discounts, returns and costs.
2. Focus stock and promotions on the top-profit products.
3. Encourage UPI and prepaid payments over COD.
4. Work on improving the overall profit margin.
5. Grow sales in Uttar Pradesh and Delhi, where sales are lower than in Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh.
